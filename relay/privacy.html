Relay Newsroom

Privacy Policy

Effective: September 18, 2026

Overview

Relay Newsroom is a self-hosted news channel with a synthetic presenter. It reads public news feeds, renders short bulletins, and can publish them as Reels to an Instagram account and send approval requests over WhatsApp. It is operated by its owner for the owner's own accounts. This policy explains what data the software and its Meta app handle, and how.

Who this applies to

The Meta app connected to Relay Newsroom is used only by the person running the software, on that person's own Instagram professional account and WhatsApp number. It is not offered to the public, does not have other users, and does not collect information about anyone who views the published content.

Data the app handles

  • Instagram: an access token, the connected account's id and username, and the ids and links of Reels the software has published. These are used only to publish to that account and to show, in the operator's own control room, what was published.
  • WhatsApp: messages exchanged between the software and the operator's own phone number: approval requests it sends, and the replies and commands the operator sends back. Messages from any other number are discarded without being processed.
  • News content: headlines, summaries, links and pictures from public RSS feeds and article pages, which are the material of the bulletins.

No personal data about third parties is collected. The software does not read the Instagram account's followers, messages, comments or insights, and does not access any WhatsApp conversations other than the operator's own.

Where data is stored

Everything is stored on the operator's own server, in a local database and local files. Access tokens are kept only there, are never sent to a browser, and are never shared with any other service. Nothing is stored on servers run by the author of the software.

Sharing

Data is shared only with Meta's own services, as needed to publish a Reel or send a WhatsApp message through the official APIs, and with ElevenLabs when its voice service is used to read a bulletin's text aloud. No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone else.

Retention and deletion

Tokens and account details are kept for as long as the operator keeps the accounts connected, and are erased when the operator disconnects them in the control room or deletes the local database. Published Reels remain on Instagram until removed there. The operator can delete all data at any time by removing the software's data directory.

Data deletion requests

Because the app has no users other than its operator, there is no third-party data to delete. If you believe the software holds data about you, email the address below and it will be removed and confirmed.

Analytics and tracking

The software contains no analytics, tracking pixels or telemetry.

Changes to this policy

Changes will be published on this page with a new effective date.
