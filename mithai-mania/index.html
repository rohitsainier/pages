Mithai Mania

A festive match-3 roguelike for iPhone

How to play

  • Swipe a sweet (or tap two neighbours) to swap them and line up 3 or more of a kind.
  • Match 4 to make a line blaster, which clears its whole row or column when matched.
  • Match 5 to make a Diwali bomb. Swap it with any sweet to eat every sweet of that kind.
  • Reach each level's target before your moves run out. Unused moves become bonus points.
  • After every level, pick a treat. Treats stack for the rest of the run.
  • Fail a level and the run is over. Try to beat your best score!

Questions

Does the game need the internet?

No. Mithai Mania works completely offline.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. There are no ads, no purchases and no accounts.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Use the Sound and Haptics toggles on the home screen, or pause a run and change them there. Sounds also follow your iPhone's silent switch.

How do I reset my best score?

Your records are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting and reinstalling the app resets them.
