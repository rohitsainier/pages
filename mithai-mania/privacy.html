Privacy Policy

Mithai Mania for iPhone · Effective 25 September 2026

Short version: Mithai Mania does not collect, share or sell any personal information. It works fully offline and has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What the app stores

To remember your progress, the app saves a few things only on your device:

  • your best score, best level and number of runs played
  • a running count of sweets eaten
  • your settings (sound and haptics on or off)

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the app does not do

  • No sign-in or account of any kind
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No analytics, crash reporting services or tracking
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera
  • No network connections: the game is entirely offline

Children

Mithai Mania is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
