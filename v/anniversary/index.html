My dearest Jaan,

This week wasn’t easy for either of us. We fought more than we should have — and mostly because of me. And still, today, as we complete two months, you are here with me. I can never thank you enough for that.

I didn’t even know what love meant. You made me feel that a person can love someone this much. You made my life this easy, this peaceful, this calm — otherwise I would have just worked myself into the ground.

I respect you so much. You stay in pain yourself and still get everyone else’s work done, still wish good for everyone. Not everyone has a heart like that.

Thank you for choosing me. Nobody else understands me — and nobody has ever put in the effort you have.

Happy two months, my wife. And here is my promise to you — I am going to be better. Not only to make you happy, but so that you never feel again the way I made you feel.

Yours, always —
